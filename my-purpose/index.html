My purpose

i believe that everything that happens to me has khayr meaning in it. i do not believe that any goal is too big nor too small, with the mercy of god, the world is for the taking. i know that i will not be receiving anything more or less than what is written for me. with that being said, my objective is to take the skills that i have been given and use them to create and bring beautiful intentions into this world, and use them to please god in the most exceptional manner possible. i don't believe in trying to endlessly grind. i believe in praying and maximizing barakah.
