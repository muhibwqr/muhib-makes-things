i'm muhib, a 19 year old from toronto. if you asked my mom about me, she would tell you i grew up obsessed with fast cars, traveling, selling sweet treats, and caring deeply for those around me.

being homeschooled gave me the agency to just build and figure things out without fear of failing. i designed for a startup at eleven, scaled software to thousands, ran an agency, and lost more than i earned. every venture taught me to move fast, take the loss, and step up for my community.

what i care about underneath all of it is complex systems and designing the future. i am ambitious and not intimidated by big ideas, because i believe you only get exactly what is written for you.

through all the noise, i am anchored by ihsan. i want to build with excellence, extreme benevolence, deep technical care, and style, to bring beautiful things into this world and serve his creation. inshallah. i am working to be remembered, not known.
