muhib waqar | محب وقار

assalamu‑alaykum · peace be upon you

i'm muhib, a 19 year old from toronto. ask my mom and she would tell you i grew up obsessed with fast cars, traveling, selling sweet treats, and caring deeply for the people around me.

homeschooling gave me the high agency to just build and not fear failing. my curiosity ran everywhere: creative writing, keyboards, marketing, video editing, woodworking, hiking, medicine, research. i designed for a startup at eleven and scaled software to thousands, and at 17 i ran a social media agency for realtors, partly to ride around in their corvettes and lambos.

i tried a lot, failed more, earned a little, lost more, and learned to move fast, take the loss, and step up for my community. all of it pointed me at complex systems and designing the future. i am ambitious and not intimidated by big ideas, because i believe you only get exactly what is written for you.

through all the noise, i am anchored by ihsan. i want to build with excellence, extreme benevolence, deep technical care, and style, to bring beautiful things into this world and serve his creation. inshallah. i am working to be remembered, not known.
