ihsan as a spec

building as if it is seen · 3 min

there is a hadith i keep coming back to. the angel asks the prophet, peace be upon him, what ihsan is. he answers: to worship god as if you see him, and though you do not see him, he sees you.

i think about that line as someone who builds. most things get made to pass a test. to ship. to look right to whoever opens the pull request. ihsan is a different spec entirely. it is building as if the one who misses nothing is the one reviewing.

that changes the parts no one checks. the function buried four files deep. the edge case that fires once a year. the variable you could have called x and instead named the true thing. ihsan is the quality of the work where no human is watching, because the whole premise is that someone always is.

benevolence is part of the spec

ihsan is not only excellence. it is also doing more good than is owed. in software that is the hundred milliseconds you hand back to a stranger you will never meet. the error message that treats the reader like a person having a hard day. the default that quietly protects someone who will never open the docs.

this is slower. it is supposed to be. comfort is not the point, and shipping fast is not the whole point. care is the point, and care shows up exactly in the places that do not show.

i want to be remembered, not known. ihsan is how i think you get there. you stop building for the eye that blinks and start building for the one that does not, and somehow the work that comes out is the kind people remember anyway.

build as if it is seen. it is.
