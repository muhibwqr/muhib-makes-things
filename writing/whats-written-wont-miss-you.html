what's written won't miss you

qadr, contentment, and your own path · 2 min

there is a line i keep close: what is written for you will not miss you, and what missed you was never written for you.

it sounds like comfort, but it is really a release. it means i can unclench. i do the work, all of it, and then i let the outcome belong to the one who wrote it. the thing meant for me cannot be taken from me. the thing taken was never mine to begin with.

someone else's path

that is also why i never have to envy anyone. there will always be someone with a different journey, a different path, a different story. their portion is theirs, written long before either of us arrived. mine is mine. we are not even running the same race, so there is nothing of theirs to lose and nothing of mine for them to take.

so i run my own line, hard and open-handed. what is for me will find me, even if it takes the long way around. inshallah.
