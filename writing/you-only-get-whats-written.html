you only get what's written

qadr, agency, and ambition · 3 min

i am ambitious in a way that makes some people uncomfortable. i also believe, fully, that you only get exactly what is written for you. said out loud those sound like opposites. i think they are the same sentence.

in islam there is qadr, the divine decree, and the qalam, the pen that wrote what would be before it was. and there is tawakkul: you do the work, then you hand the outcome back to god.

the outcome was never my job

here is what that does to someone who builds. if the result is already written, then the result is not my responsibility. the effort is. i am accountable for the striking, not the landing. that is the most freeing thing i know.

it lets me move fast and take a loss without the loss touching my center. i have earned a little and lost more, and none of it moved the floor i stand on, because the floor was never the win. it was the trying, done well, for him.

being homeschooled taught me to build without asking permission. faith taught me to build without fear of the result. agency, aimed by trust. one without the other is either anxiety or laziness. together they are the only way i know how to work.

so i go all the way out, and i hold the result loosely. you only get what is written. so write hard, and let the rest be his. inshallah.
