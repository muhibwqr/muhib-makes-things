About

In the words of the Prophet, peace be upon him, on ihsan: worship God as if you see Him, and though you do not see Him, He sees you. For me, better or worse, building is worship. I am a mathematics student at the University of Waterloo. I study vision-language-action models, build an AI realtor at Zown, and host events with Pillars Network.

I wrestled for three years in high school. My best result was fourth at the Ontario Opens — a tournament that taught me more about losing with dignity than any win did. The mat is where I first learned that repetition, weight class, and showing up beat talent on a good day.

Before this I sold sneakers, refurbished computers, ran a clothing brand, coded games, did voice acting, and chased nine million views across my own socials. What stayed constant: I want to create benevolently, and have the work reflect eloquence, grandeur, and beauty. Honoring the elegance of God's design means striving for excellence in whatever I ship.

I am in Toronto most days — probably exploring, studying, or making something. You can find me on GitHub and X: @muhibwqr. Email: notesfrommuhib@gmail.com.
